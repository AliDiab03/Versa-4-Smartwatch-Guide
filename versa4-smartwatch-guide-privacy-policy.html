Privacy Policy

Versa 4 Smartwatch Guide · Package: com.versa4smartwatchguide.app
Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Versa 4 Smartwatch Guide (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App on your Android device. The App is an unofficial companion guide that provides setup help, tips, specifications and troubleshooting information for Fitbit Versa 4.

In short: we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The only third-party data collection comes from Google AdMob, which shows ads in the App.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not require registration or login and does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files or phone number. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device, such as your progress inside the app, such as daily water counter and checklist items you tick off, and whether you have completed the first-run welcome screen. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, including banner ads, interstitial ads and app open ads. To provide and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (the Google advertising identifier) and related device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks, and diagnostic information

This information is collected by Google and is handled under Google’s own policies. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The App may use the Android advertising ID (AD_ID permission) for advertising. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). You can also manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

4. Internet access

The App uses the internet to load ads and to open web pages or documents you choose to view, such as official support pages, video searches or store links. The App also checks your network status to avoid loading content when you are offline.

5. Third-party links and trademarks

The App contains links to external websites, such as manufacturer support pages, online retailers and video platforms. These sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their content or practices. Please review their policies before sharing any information.

Versa 4 Smartwatch Guide is an independent, unofficial guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Fitbit and Google. All product names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify the products the guide is about.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information on our own servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Locally stored data is protected by Android’s app sandbox. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to keep the App safe.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Clear or reset the App’s data from Android settings at any time.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
